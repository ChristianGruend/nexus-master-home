          <div class="article-body"><p>Sakurada Fumiriya is... In December 2018, Sakurada Fumiriya appeared in VRoid Studio[v0.5.0] introduction movie at the time of its release, as an example of male model. The origin of his name is a certain famous building in Spain.</p>
<p><iframe src="//www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/KQU2FpfG3NI" width="560" height="315" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen=""></iframe></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h3><strong>■ Sakurada Fumiriya's profile</strong></h3>
<h3>・<a href="https://hub.vroid.com/characters/6912965120285194650/models/2261505926203110716" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sakurada Fumiriya's page on VRoid Hub</a></h3>
<h3>In December 2018, a male body was added to VRoid Studio[v0.5.0] and it appeared in the introduction video at the time of its release. His key features are his blonde, semi-long hair and his gentle smile. His father is a craftsman from a building company dating back more than a century, and Fumiriya himself is a returnee who's been learning about the traditional techniques of Japanese architecture. He's the cousin of the Sendagaya sisters (his mother is their aunt).</h3>
<h3>・Sendagaya sisters</h3>
<h3>　- S<a href="https://vroid.pixiv.help/hc/en-us/articles/360013482714" target="_blank" rel="noopener">endagaya Shino</a></h3>
<h3>　- <a href="https://vroid.pixiv.help/hc/en-us/articles/360012381793" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sendagaya Shibu</a></h3>
